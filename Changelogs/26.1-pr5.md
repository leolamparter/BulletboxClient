# Bulletbox 26.1 Pre-Release 5
With this pre-release we are adding some of the final features to Bulletbox. Release Candidates will be posted soon.

- Updated to Raylib 8.0.0.
- Raiders' iron swords have been replaced with wooden swords and a 5% chance of a stone one, as they were too OP.
- Raid Outposts' minimum distance has been lowered.
- Added a skin selector screen, with 2 skins: Bob (Default) and Bob's Friend.
- Added a new special skin for the Apex Challenge reward.
- Added scorpions, spawning in groups randomly, in deserts, beaches, and mesas.
- Scorpions' now have a 300 millisecond time of having an "attacking" texture upon damaging (or attenmpting to damage) the player.
- All entities' textures are now flipped horizontally when moving to the right to make their textures properly rotate.
- Cut all weapons' cooldowns down in half.
- Added raid patrols, having 2-3 raiders, which spawn randomly over the world.
- You can now open raid outpost chests whenever.
- Added a mob cap of 6.
- Raids have been removed.
- Changed the chance of mobs fleeing at low health from 1% per tick to 5% per raider.
- Scorpions now have a 15% chance of replacing raidshroomers in raid patrols.
- Removed the raid boss bar and countdown.
- Mobs outside your render distance now despawn after being out of render for over 8 seconds.
- "Right click to open" now shows above outposts always, until opening.
- Added dashing with left shift, costing 10 hunger points.
- **All** mobs now avoid lava.
- The client side now removes mobs upon death by lava.
- Updated the textures for raid outposts and raidshroomers.
- Added textures for scorpions.
- Added sound effect for raidshroomers.
- Added `UnloadTextures()` to the Player class to prevent GPU overload.
- Raid outposts now dissapear with a cloud of smoke upon being emptied.
- Raid outposts now spawn with idle 1-3 raiders.
- Opening raid outposts now aggro nearby raiders.
- Hurting raiders now aggro nearby raiders.
- Dying now downgrades tools and weapons and keeps your shield. all other items get deleted, including all wooden weapons (and stone kanabo, as there is no wooden variant)
