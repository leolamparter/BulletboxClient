# Bulletbox 26.1.1-03c
## The Volcanic & Ethereal Update

# New Biomes (26.1.1-03c.1)
- **Ashen Wastelands:** Introduced a desolate, ash-covered biome characterized by dark, muted colors and ambient ash fall particles. (26.1.1-03c.1/1)
- **Lava Pools:** Added dangerous lava pool biomes, featuring glowing ember particles and inflicting damage to players standing within them. (26.1.1-03c.1/2)
- Both biomes now feature unique ember particle effects that spawn from the player's footsteps and ambiently in the world. (26.1.1-03c.1/3)

# New Adversaries (26.1.1-03c.2)
- **Brimstalker Boss:** Introduced the formidable Brimstalker boss, a large, aggressive mob that utilizes bomb attacks and a powerful charge ability. (26.1.1-03c.2/1)
- **Flicker Mob:** Added the elusive Flicker mob, a smaller, ethereal entity that teleports when damaged and performs a charge attack. Flickers are now spawned rarely during the night. (26.1.1-03c.2/2)
- Both Brimstalker and Flicker mobs have unique idle, angry, and afraid textures, and their rendering is scaled appropriately (Flicker is 50% smaller). (26.1.1-03c.2/3)

# AI & Gameplay (26.1.1-03c.3)
- Brimstalker and Flicker mobs now utilize a multi-phase charge attack system, including a telegraph phase where they back away before lunging. (26.1.1-03c.3/1)
- Brimstalker mobs now throw bombs at players, which home in slightly and explode on impact or after a timer. (26.1.1-03c.3/2)
